## Camera, lighting and editing

- Framing: shot size medium close-up 100%; subject is the in-focus region (no face; shot size is an estimate)
- Camera: moving camera (gimbal / handheld pan) 100%; typical move push-in
- Lighting: soft frontal light (flat light). Key light frontal (from the camera side; both sides of the subject about equally bright), at subject height; lighting ratio about 1.2:1 (0.2 stops); soft light (almost no lit / shadow edge); colour temperature warm (about 3000–4000K); background clearly blurred (shallow depth of field / portrait mode)
- Grade: vintage film (lifted blacks, warm); high saturation, low contrast, lifted blacks (greyish shadows), overall warm
- Editing: 27.0 cuts per minute, 1.97s average live-action shot; 1 camera set-up(s) / location(s); 0 jump cut(s) and 0 punch-in(s) within the same set-up; inserts (screenshots / screen recordings / cards / stock) take 96%

**Shooting plan for the remake**

1. Framing: mostly medium close-up
2. Camera: moving camera (gimbal / handheld pan) 100%; typical move push-in
3. Lighting: soft frontal light (flat light) — a large source from the camera side, almost no shadow on the subject: facing a window, a ring light, or a big softbox behind the camera
4. Editing: 27.0 cuts per minute, 1.97s average live-action shot; 1 camera set-up(s) / location(s); 0 jump cut(s) and 0 punch-in(s) within the same set-up; inserts (screenshots / screen recordings / cards / stock) take 96%

Gear:

- white desk or white cards to bounce; shadows go lighter
- Note: background clearly blurred (shallow depth of field / portrait mode)
