# Paper-Diorama Documentary Direction

Read only when the brief calls for a cinematic paper world. This is an alternative
to flat mixed-media collage; do not replace an approved reference just because
the alternative permits more dramatic camera movement.

## Material and composition

Build a miniature world from layered cardboard, aged paper and restrained
newsprint texture. Use a limited palette, a recurring prop, readable depth and
one focal action. Sepia with a burnt-orange accent is a starting option. Match
the user's supplied palette when it differs.

Keep figures and objects stylistically coherent. Anonymous archival cutouts can
be useful supporting elements, but they are not evidence of a historical event.
Separate verified facts, explanatory metaphors and illustrative imagery.

## Style-key prompt

```text
Cinematic editorial paper-diorama style reference for [TOPIC]:
layered [paper/cardboard material], torn edges, subtle halftone print,
[approved palette] with [accent prop], handcrafted miniature geometry,
warm directional light, soft readable shadows and controlled macro depth.
Show [recurring prop] consistently in a close detail and a wider setting.
One clear focal point with space for editable labels. No readable text,
numbers, logos, watermark or live-action footage.
```

## Reusable prop record

For each important prop, retain its real source/reference file, appearance,
relative size, function in the explanation and confirmed media identifier if
uploaded. Keep those references through regeneration. A prop asset from a past
run is usable only if the user has supplied or authorized that actual asset.

## Scene prompt

```text
STYLE: Match the approved paper-diorama reference, palette and recurring prop.
START: [spatial arrangement and subject the viewer should notice first].
ACTION: [one causal change] followed by [visible consequence].
CAMERA: [one purposeful move], revealing [new relationship] after [readable action].
END: [stable focal position or movement carried into the next shot].
AUDIO: [approved sound mode]; no generated dialogue when narration is separate.
NEGATIVE: [mechanism errors], inconsistent scale/material, malformed geometry,
gibberish text, logos and watermark.
```

A continuous-looking camera move is optional. Use a dive, whip or impact only
when it improves the explanation and survives review. Do not mandate speed ramps
or obscure a cause with blur. A clear cut can preserve spatial understanding.

## Exact labels and sound

Letterpress labels can suit paper props; use editable composited text for exact
wording and verify perspective, contrast and occlusion. Do not ask generation to
invent numerical data or rely on malformed lettering.

Paper movement, soft impacts and mechanical sounds can support the material.
Choose them from authorized audio assets or a tool that actually supports those
sounds, then mix beneath narration. Silence is valid for a requested silent stage.

## Inspection

Check prop identity, contact with surfaces, light direction, action order, focus
and the transition into the next scene. If the idea becomes unclear at macro
scale, widen the framing rather than adding more visual events. Update only the
failing scene within the requested scope and preserve the approved style.
