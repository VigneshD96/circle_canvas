## 1. The story in one line

Mom runs out of chai powder → she sends her son to the corner shop with money → he buys it → the shopkeeper gives him ₹1 change → mom lets him keep the coin → he stares at it, already picturing the toffee it will buy. End card: *"forgotten little joys of life (episode 1)"*.

The joke in the fixed caption: **"POV: you were your mom's Blinkit and Zepto"**. Blinkit and Zepto are India's 10-minute grocery delivery apps, so the kid *was* the quick-delivery service. The emotional payoff is the change.

## 3. Animation type (measured)

- **Cut-out ("puppet") 2D animation over hand-drawn illustrations**, not frame-by-frame redrawing.
- Props and limbs move as **rigid pieces on keyframed paths**: the hand rising to the tin, coins dropping into the palm. The hand in shot 2 updates on twos (about 12 new positions a second).
- Big expression or pose changes are **single-frame swaps** between drawings: fist → open palm, worried face → grin, eyes open → shut.
- Between swaps there is constant small "life" (about 15–22 changed frames a second): flames, sparkles, small sways, slides. The background edges barely change (corner difference ≈ 0.6/255). There's **no camera move**; framing is set by the drawing.
- Some beats are **complete stills** for 1.5 s (shot 5), used for comic timing.
- Film-style effects: a focus pull (shot 13), a "thought" cross-dissolve (shot 18), sparkle stars on coins.

## 7. Why it works (the formula)
1. **Relatable hook caption.** It names a shared childhood role through a modern joke ("you were your mom's Blinkit and Zepto").
2. **Ordinary home problem → child's errand → tiny reward.** The reward is the emotional point: the ₹1 change.
3. **Wordless storytelling** with cultural props any Indian viewer recognises in a frame.
4. **Quiet first, music on the turn.** The track drops in exactly when the kid sets off.
5. **Slow down at the end:** long holds on faces and an imagined treat.
6. **Series branding end card** ("episode 1") turns one video into a collectible series. The post caption asks a question to pull comments ("What did you buy with the change?").
