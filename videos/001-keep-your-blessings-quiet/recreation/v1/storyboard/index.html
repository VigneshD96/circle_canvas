Style rules carried over from the style bible
  • Dark-brown tapered outlines on the character only, turning orange on edges the sun hits
  • Two-tone soft cel shading with a rim-light band on the lit side; very warm orange skin
  • Big eyes with highlights, thick upper lashes with outer flicks, freckles and blush, nose shown as a tone
  • Messy high top-knot bun with flyaways, black spaghetti-strap crop cami, high-waisted dark-wash jeans
  • Painted, unlined, softly blurred background; three depth layers (road, her, the world behind)
Choices I made that you didn't specify
  • White sneakers. The reel never shows her shoes outdoors.
  • The original two-line title sits above the picture, as in the reel.
  • Her body is in profile and her face is turned three-quarters toward the camera, so both eyes show, as in the reel.
